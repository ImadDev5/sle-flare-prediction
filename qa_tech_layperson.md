# Q&A for Tech-Savvy Layperson: TAGT Project

## Simple Questions

**Q1: What is TAGT?**
A: TAGT is a computer program that uses artificial intelligence to predict if a person with a disease called SLE (a type of autoimmune disease) will get worse soon.

**Q2: Why is this important?**
A: If doctors know early that a patient might get worse, they can give treatment sooner and help the patient stay healthy.

**Q3: What kind of data does TAGT use?**
A: It looks at information from genes, how proteins in the body interact, and health scores from the doctor, all over time.

**Q4: How does TAGT work?**
A: It uses special AI techniques to find patterns in the data. It pays attention to important changes in the patient's health history and how different body parts are connected.

**Q5: Is TAGT better than other computer programs?**
A: Yes, it is more accurate than older methods because it combines many types of information and uses advanced AI tricks.

## Deeper Questions

**Q6: What is a Graph Neural Network?**
A: It's a type of AI that understands connections, like a social network but for proteins in your body.

**Q7: What is Attention in AI?**
A: It means the computer focuses more on important parts of the data, like remembering the most important events in your life.

**Q8: What is Multi-modal Fusion?**
A: It's when the computer combines different types of information (like text, numbers, and pictures) to make a better decision.

**Q9: What are the future plans for TAGT?**
A: The team wants to:
- Test it with more people and in more hospitals
- Make it easier for doctors to use
- Add more types of data, like pictures or lifestyle info
- Make the computer explain its answers better

## In Simple Indian English

TAGT is a smart computer program that helps doctors know if a patient with SLE will get worse soon. It looks at many things together—genes, proteins, and health scores over time. It uses special AI tricks to give early warning. In future, it will become even better and easier for doctors to use.
